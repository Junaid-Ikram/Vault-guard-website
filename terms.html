1. Zero-Knowledge Architecture & Encryption Scope

VaultGuard provides zero-knowledge password and digital identity management. All encryption (AES-256-GCM with 96-bit unique IVs) and key derivation (PBKDF2 with HMAC-SHA256 and 600,000 iterations) occurs exclusively on your local device hardware before any data is saved or transmitted.

VaultGuard developers, cloud operators, and automated services possess zero mathematical ability to decrypt, read, or inspect your credentials, card details, or notes under any circumstances.

2. Master Password & 24-Word Recovery Key Sole Custody

You maintain sole custody and responsibility for your Master Password and emergency 24-word Recovery Key. Because VaultGuard employs strict zero-knowledge cryptography, lost credentials cannot be recovered, bypassed, or reset by customer support. If both your Master Password and Recovery Key are lost, encrypted vault contents are permanently irretrievable.

4. Anti-Sabotage Clause (Local Reset vs. Cloud Isolation)

Executing "Reset Vault & Start Fresh" or clearing local application data strictly purges local device storage and rotates local device identity on that specific hardware unit. A local reset does NOT delete or compromise entries stored in your cloud vault. Any vault subsequently initialized on a reset device begins in an isolated state and cannot overwrite prior cloud data without authenticated credentials.

5. Multi-Device Trust & Device Revocation

Connecting multiple devices establishes cryptographically verified hardware identity (ECDSA P-256) and ephemeral key exchanges (ECDH P-256). You maintain the unrestricted right to revoke any lost, stolen, or untrusted hardware from any authorized device at any time. Revocation triggers instant rotation of the Vault Encryption Key (VEK), locking out the compromised device permanently.
