Cryptographic Standards

Zero-Knowledge Cryptographic Architecture

Designed according to Zero Trust principles: all cryptographic operations take place client-side so our cloud infrastructure never receives unencrypted data.

Engineered for Zero-Knowledge Defense

Every stored record is individually sealed on your hardware device using AES-256 in Galois/Counter Mode with 96-bit initialization vectors (IVs) and canonical Associated Authenticated Data (AAD). Master passwords undergo 600,000 PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 iterations to resist offline brute-force attacks.

  • AES-256-GCM Authenticated Encryption

    The industry benchmark for symmetric cryptography, guaranteeing confidentiality and tamper-evident authentication for every record.

  • PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (600,000 Iterations)

    Exceeds NIST recommendations for key derivation, effectively safeguarding against dictionary and GPU cracking clusters.

  • ECDSA P-256 Device Identity & Ephemeral ECDH

    Asymmetric hardware keys authenticate enrolled devices and enable instant Vault Encryption Key (VEK) rotation if a device is revoked.

  • Zero-Plaintext Multi-Device Sync

    Cloud datastores receive exclusively encrypted ciphertext blobs, IVs, and version counters — no plaintext ever leaves your device.
