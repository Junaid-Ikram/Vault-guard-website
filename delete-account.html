Account & Data Deletion Request

Application: VaultGuard-Password Manager • Developer: VaultGuard Security Engineering / Junaid Ikram • Last Updated: October 2026

📱 In-App Steps ✉️ Email Request 🗑️ Data Deleted 🛡️ Retained Data ⏱️ Retention Policy 💬 Contact

1. Overview & User Sovereignty

At VaultGuard-Password Manager, privacy is our core architectural commitment. In full compliance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion policies, GDPR (Article 17 — Right to Erasure), and CCPA, every user maintains complete, unilateral control over their cloud account and data.

You may permanently delete your cloud account, all cryptographic identities, and all cloud-stored vault records at any time with zero cooldowns or mandatory waiting periods.

2. How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You have two convenient methods to execute permanent deletion:

📱 Method 1: Inside the VaultGuard App (Instant — Recommended)

If you have the VaultGuard app installed on your Android device:

  1. Open VaultGuard and unlock your vault using your Master Password or Biometrics.
  2. Tap the Settings tab (gear icon) in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Select Cloud & Multi-Device Sync.
  4. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section and tap "Delete Cloud Account & All Data".
  5. Enter your cloud account password when prompted to authenticate ownership.
  6. Confirm the permanent deletion dialog.

⚡ Outcome: Your cloud authentication account, Firestore vault ciphertext, device trust keys, and session cookies are purged immediately from Google Cloud servers.

✉️ Method 2: Web & Email Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or no longer have access to your mobile device:

  1. Send an email directly to: junaidikram17@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: "VaultGuard Account Deletion Request".
  3. Provide the registered email address used for your VaultGuard cloud sync account.
  4. Our compliance engineering team will verify ownership and trigger complete cloud erasure within 48 hours.

📩 You will receive an automated confirmation email once your user ID and all associated server records have been purged.

3. What Data Is Permanently Deleted?

Upon executing an account deletion request, the following records are permanently and irretrievably destroyed:

  • Authentication Identity: Firebase Authentication user record, UID, registered email, and authentication tokens.
  • Cloud Vault Documents: All encrypted ciphertext envelopes, initialization vectors (IVs), salt values, and item metadata stored in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Hardware Device Trust Records: Asymmetric ECDSA public keys, enrolled device UUIDs, device pairing envelopes, and rotation logs.
  • Subscription Linkage: RevenueCat customer identification records and entitlement links, reverting your profile state to a completely unlinked account.

4. What Data Is Retained?

Zero user credentials, passwords, or personal data are retained. Because VaultGuard utilizes zero-knowledge client-side encryption (AES-256-GCM), we never held plaintext passwords, credentials, notes, or payment card details on our servers in the first place.

⚠️ Google Play Subscription Notice: Deleting your VaultGuard account destroys all server data, but does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription managed by Google. To prevent recurring charges from Google, please open Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & Subscriptions → Subscriptions and cancel your VaultGuard Pro subscription.

5. Data Retention Period

Retention Period: 0 Days (Immediate).

  • In-App Deletion: Takes effect immediately and synchronously.
  • Email Requests: Processed and verified within a maximum of 48 hours.
  • Backup Lifecycles: Ephemeral server backup caches undergo automatic purging in accordance with Google Cloud Firestore standard backup rotation (maximum 30 days).
