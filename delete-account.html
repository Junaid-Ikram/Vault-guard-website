Account & Data Deletion Request

App: VaultGuard-Password Manager • Developer: VaultGuard Security Engineering / Junaid Ikram • Last Updated: October 2026

Overview

At VaultGuard-Password Manager, we are committed to complete user sovereignty and full compliance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion policies, GDPR, and CCPA. Users have the absolute right to permanently delete their account and all associated cloud data at any time.

How to Delete Your Account and Data

You can request account deletion using either of the following two methods:

Method 1: Directly Inside the App (Instant)

If you have the VaultGuard app installed on your device:

  1. Open VaultGuard on your Android device and unlock your vault.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (gear icon) in the navigation bar.
  3. Select Cloud & Multi-Device Sync.
  4. Scroll down to the danger zone and tap "Delete Cloud Account & All Data".
  5. Enter your cloud account password to confirm your identity.
  6. Confirm the deletion prompt. Your account and cloud records are immediately and permanently erased from Google Cloud servers.

Method 2: Web / Email Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device:

  1. Send an email to our data privacy team at: junaidikram17@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: "VaultGuard Account Deletion Request".
  3. Include the email address registered with your VaultGuard Cloud account.
  4. Our automated systems will verify account ownership and execute complete deletion of all records within 48 hours. You will receive an email confirmation upon completion.

What Data Is Permanently Deleted?

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently erased and cannot be recovered:

  • Firebase Authentication Account: Your cloud user identity, email, authentication tokens, and profile records.
  • Cloud Vault Documents: All encrypted ciphertext envelopes, device records, and sync logs stored in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Device Trust Tokens: Hardware enrollment public keys and device pairing cryptographic envelopes.
  • Subscription Linkage: App user ID associations in RevenueCat are terminated, resetting your cloud profile to a clean slate.

What Data Is Retained?

Zero user credentials or vault items are retained. Because VaultGuard uses zero-knowledge end-to-end encryption, we never had access to your plaintext passwords, cards, or notes in the first place, and all encrypted cloud storage is purged immediately.

Note on Google Play Subscriptions: Deleting your VaultGuard account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. To stop upcoming billing cycles from Google, please manage or cancel your active subscription in Google Play Store under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Data Retention Period

Retention Period: 0 Days (Immediate). Deletion requests executed in-app take effect instantly. Deletion requests submitted via email are verified and executed within a maximum of 48 hours.
